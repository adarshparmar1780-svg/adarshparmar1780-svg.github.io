Satya Scan · Case study

Overview

Satya Scan is a compliance-oriented application for reviewing packaged commodity labels against the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011. The live app requires sign-in before its workspace can be inspected.

Problem

Inspectors need to review multiple declarations and presentation requirements on packaging. Manual checking can be slow and difficult to record consistently.

Approach

Bring image review, extracted label information and rule-based validation into one workflow, with reviewable evidence and a record of previous inspections.

Key features

Product image scanning, OCR and declaration extraction, missing declaration checks, font and readability analysis, violation summaries, evidence, searchable product history, dashboard and role-based access. The brief also describes PDF and editable report exports.

Workflow

Conceptual product path based on the supplied project description.

Product imageImage processingText extractionDeclaration detectionRule validationReadability analysisCompliance reviewReport & history

Technical architecture

The implementation is described as an OCR and rule-based compliance system. Its public GitHub repository is not available, and the live app is behind sign-in, so specific frameworks and services are not stated here.

Challenges

Label content can vary in placement and legibility. Useful automation needs to make extracted text and potential issues reviewable instead of treating every detection as a final decision.

Future improvements

Possible next steps include clearer evidence-to-rule traceability, broader label coverage and review tools that make uncertain OCR results easy to correct.

Impact & live demo

Designed to make compliance checking more systematic, searchable and reportable while reducing repetitive verification. No official deployment, production scale or accuracy figures are claimed.
